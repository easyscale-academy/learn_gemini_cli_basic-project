# Learn to Choose Your Gemini Model

## Objective

Learn how to switch between Gemini models (Pro, Flash, Flash-Lite) to optimize your workflow for cost, speed, and capability. Understanding model selection is essential for working efficiently with Gemini CLI.

Read the tutorial: [Choosing Your Gemini Model](https://github.com/easyscale-academy/learn_gemini_cli_basic-project/tree/08-Choose-Model/)

## Actionable Items

1. Read the tutorial in `README.md`
2. Open Gemini CLI in your terminal
3. Use the `/model` command to switch to Flash
4. Verify your selection by asking Gemini a simple question
5. Open `.gemini/settings.json` to see where the setting is stored
6. (Optional) Try launching Gemini with `gemini --model gemini-2.5-flash` to see the command-line override

**Estimated time:** 5-10 minutes
